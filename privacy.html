Privacy Policy

Skunked: Way of the Spray — Last updated: March 9, 2026

Summary

We do not operate our own analytics or user-account system, but the game may display advertising. Skunked: Way of the Spray runs on your device and stores gameplay progress locally. When ads are shown, Google advertising services may collect or process device, browser, app, and ad-interaction data subject to their own policies.

What Data We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information, gameplay telemetry, or account data on our own servers through the game client.

Local Storage

The game uses your browser's localStorage to save:

  • High scores
  • Achievement progress
  • Display and performance preferences (e.g., mobile performance mode)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Advertising

The web version may display Google AdSense ads, and the Android app may display Google AdMob ads. Those services may use cookies, mobile identifiers, IP address, approximate location, diagnostic data, and ad interaction data to serve, limit, and measure ads.

If you use the game through a browser or mobile app store build, Google may process data under its own privacy terms. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Cookies

We do not set our own advertising cookies, but third-party ad providers may set cookies or use equivalent technologies when ads are displayed in the web version.

Third-Party Services

The game may integrate Google advertising services to display ads. If the game is hosted on a platform such as Netlify or Vercel, those providers may also collect standard web server logs (IP address, request URL, user agent) as part of their hosting infrastructure. Refer to the relevant provider privacy policies for details.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any data, the game is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or any other age).

Changes to This Policy

If we change how ads, storage, or data handling work, we will update this page with a new "Last updated" date.
