1. Who We Are

Skunked: Way of the Spray ("Game") is developed and published by Mephitideus Interactive ("we", "us", "our"). The Game is available as a web application at skunked.io and as an Android application via Google Play. This Privacy Policy applies to all versions of the Game.

For privacy matters, the data controller is Mephitideus Interactive. Contact details are in Section 13.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process a limited amount of information needed to operate game features and monetization. We do not request or process sensitive Android permissions such as call logs or SMS for account verification.

Data you actively submit

  • Leaderboard submissions: when you submit a score, we process your short player name/initials, score, selected game stats (such as level, achievement count, prestige), and a technical run identifier used to prevent duplicate submissions.
  • Purchase entitlement sync: on Android, when enabled in game, we process your Google Play Games player ID and entitlement state (for example, ad-free ownership) to support cross-device restore behavior.

Analytics and product telemetry

The Game uses Google Tag Manager / Google Ads tagging on the web version and may send gameplay event telemetry (for example, game starts, level completions, ad and purchase funnel events). This helps us measure stability and product performance. You can opt out of this telemetry by setting localStorage key skunkfu_analytics_optout to 1.

Data we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not collect phone call logs or SMS message content.
  • We do not request phone-number verification via call-log permissions.
  • We do not currently integrate third-party generative AI models in the game client or backend production path.

3. Local Storage

The Game stores the following data locally in your browser's localStorage or the mobile app's local storage:

  • High scores
  • Achievement progress
  • Display and performance preferences (e.g., performance mode, volume settings)

Most local data remains on your device. If you choose to submit a leaderboard score or restore/sync an entitlement, relevant fields are transmitted to our Firebase backend and to Google services as described in this policy. You can delete local data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or app storage via your device settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

The Game displays advertisements served by Google. On the web version, Google AdSense is used; on the Android version, Google AdMob is used.

Data Google may collect

When an ad is displayed, Google's advertising SDK may collect or process:

  • IP address and approximate location (country / region)
  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID or equivalent browser identifiers)
  • Browser or app type, version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

Location data disclosures (precise vs approximate)

We do not intentionally collect or infer precise location from GPS, Wi-Fi triangulation, or similar signals in our own code paths. Google advertising services may process approximate location (such as country or region) from network signals to support ad delivery, fraud prevention, and reporting.

Cookies and similar technologies

Mephitideus Interactive does not set its own advertising cookies. However, Google's advertising services may set cookies or use equivalent technologies (e.g., pixel tags, mobile SDK identifiers) when ads are displayed in the web version. You can opt-out of personalized advertising via Google Ad Settings or by visiting optout.aboutads.info.

Google's policies

Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4b. In-App Purchases (Android)

The Android version of the Game offers an optional one-time in-app purchase titled Remove Ads (product ID remove_ads, $1.99 USD or local equivalent). The purchase removes between-stage interstitial advertisements within the Game on the device used to make the purchase, and unlocks three additional cosmetic ninja skin colours (Sapphire, Amethyst, and Steel). A fourth, exclusive Gold skin and a “Founder” status badge are granted automatically to anyone who makes the purchase before December 31, 2026 (the early-access window). Optional rewarded video ads (used to revive after a defeat) remain available and are never auto-played.

How the purchase is processed

  • Payments are handled exclusively by Google Play Billing. Mephitideus Interactive does not receive, see, or store your name, billing address, credit-card number, or other payment-instrument data.
  • Google Play provides the Game with a digital receipt indicating that the remove_ads product is owned by your Google account. The Game uses this receipt only to enable the ad-free state.
  • Your entitlement is mirrored locally on your device (localStorage key skunkfu.adFree) so the Game can apply it offline. No purchase information is transmitted to Mephitideus Interactive servers.

Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the Game or switch devices using the same Google account, you can restore your Remove Ads entitlement at any time via Settings → Restore Purchase in-game. This action queries Google Play for your purchase history. If cross-device entitlement sync is active, the app may also submit the entitlement state and your Play Games player ID to our backend as described in Section 2.

Refunds and disputes

Refund requests for in-app purchases are handled by Google Play under their refund policy. We do not process refunds directly.

Google's policies for purchases

Use of Google Play Billing is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Age Requirements & Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not play the Game.

Mephitideus Interactive does not knowingly permit children under 13 years of age to use the Game, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Game uses third-party advertising (Google AdSense / AdMob), which involves automated data collection that is not appropriate for children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child under 13 has accessed the Game, please contact us at legal@mephitideusinteractive.com. We will take prompt steps to ensure no data associated with that usage is retained by applicable third-party services to the extent we are able.

7. Your Rights — European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable local law:

  • Right of access — Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction — Request that we restrict the processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability — Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

Some rights may be exercised directly with Mephitideus Interactive for backend-held records (such as leaderboard and entitlement metadata), while advertising-related rights are often most effectively exercised directly with Google. To exercise rights over Google-held data, visit the Google Data & Privacy portal.

For any data requests directed to us, contact legal@mephitideusinteractive.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Lawful basis for processing

To the extent Mephitideus Interactive processes any personal data (e.g., contact details provided voluntarily via email), the lawful basis is legitimate interest (operating and supporting the Game) or performance of a contract. Third-party advertising services rely on their own lawful bases as described in their policies.

8. Your Rights — California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold.
  • Delete personal information we hold (subject to certain exceptions).
  • Opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information.

Mephitideus Interactive does not sell personal information. We do share device/ad identifiers with Google's advertising services for the purpose of displaying ads, which may constitute "sharing" under the CPRA. To opt out of Google's ad personalization, visit Google Ad Settings.

To submit a California privacy request, contact us at legal@mephitideusinteractive.com.

9. Data Retention

Local game data (high scores, achievements, preferences) is stored on your device indefinitely until you manually clear it via browser or app storage settings.

Leaderboard and entitlement records stored in our Firebase backend are retained while needed to provide leaderboard ranking integrity, restore paid entitlements, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation.

Data collected by Google's advertising services is subject to Google's data retention policy.

If you contact us by email, we retain correspondence for up to 2 years or as long as necessary to resolve the matter.
