Key Mechanics

Double Jump

Press jump again while airborne for a second jump. Includes a short coyote window (0.15s after walking off a ledge) so jumps still register even if you step off an edge.

Shadow Strike

A directional dash attack that covers roughly 120px and deals full attack damage. It has a very short activation window — learn to use it to punish bosses after their recovery frames and to cross gaps. Shadow Strike kills count toward their own achievement chain.

Pro tip: Shadow Strike in the direction an enemy is facing so the damage hitbox connects during their movement, not behind them. It reads cleanly even through shield enemies if you hit the side not covered by the shield bubble.

Skunk Shot

A ranged spray projectile that hits enemies at distance and applies a 5-second Skunk debuff — temporarily disabling them so they stand still and can be combo'd freely. Ammo is found as pickups scattered through each run stage. You start with zero ammo; grab pickups actively.

Pro tip: Skunk Shot a Shield Tank, then close in for melee while their bubble is down. On bosses in phase 2, a skunked debuff does not suppress them — save ammo for elite enemy clusters instead.

Combo System

Every kill extends a 2-second combo window. Chain kills faster to build the multiplier: each combo stack adds 25% to all subsequent scores (maximum 10×). Hitting multiple enemies in the same attack grants a flat +150 points per additional enemy and boosts the combo counter faster.

Golden Idols

Three hidden collectibles per run stage. Each one you grab gives an instant +30 HP and 2s of invulnerability. The bigger payoff is the stacked bonus: 1 idol = +5% speed & damage, 2 idols = +10% speed & damage, all 3 = +25% speed / +30% damage. That full-set bonus makes a noticeable difference on later boss fights.

Lives

You start with 3 lives. Extra lives have a 3% drop chance from any enemy. Lose all lives and it's game over — your score goes to the leaderboard.

Stage Walkthrough

Each world has two stages: a run stage (reach the exit) followed by a boss showdown. Golden Idol locations are listed under Chapter 5.

World 1 — Forest

1-1 Forest Outskirts

A 10,000px wide forest run. The opening vertical section ("The Tall Oak") teaches platforming before enemies get dense. The Canopy Run at the 3,600–4,500px mark is the first real combo opportunity with close platform spacing.

  • Enemies: Grunts and Shield Tanks only — a gentle difficulty curve.
  • The Great Ravine (5,200–7,000px) has two high-tier platforms at x=6,300 — use them to land the first idol of the run.
  • Grab both Skunk Shot pickups at x=2,350 and x=8,500 — you enter the boss stage with them.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Tall Oak peak, Canopy Run platform, and top of the Ravine.
1-2 Forest Showdown — Boss: KYLE

A compact 4,000px arena leading to Kyle, the Crystal Flower Chief. Grab the Speed Boost at x=900 and Damage Boost at x=2,600 before triggering the boss at x=3,200.

Kyle's special is Crystal Barrage — see the Boss Guide for full tactics.

World 2 — Skunk City

2-1 Skunk City

A 12,000px urban rooftop run. Moving platforms appear for the first time (elevator at x=800 and a horizontal platform in the Construction Zone). Speedsters and Throwers are introduced — prioritize Throwers so they don't chip your HP from range.

  • Highway Bridge (x=3,200–4,000) is the first long flat fight. Let enemies cluster, then sweep them with a single Shadow Strike for quick combo build.
  • The Billboard Run (x=7,900–9,000) uses vertically moving platforms — time your jumps and don't attack mid-jump or you'll lose momentum.
  • Grab the Damage Boost at x=9,700 before the boss trigger.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Urban District platform, Highway Bridge, Billboard rooftop.
2-2 City Showdown — Boss: IRON CLAW

Iron Claw is the first true damage sponge (8.5× HP). A vertically moving platform at x=1,950 divides the arena — use it to dodge his ground slam. Grab the Speed Boost at x=800 before engaging. His Damage Boost at x=2,500 is inside his patrol range; time a pick-up dash carefully.

World 3 — Mountain Dojo

3-1 Mountain Dojo

The longest run so far at 15,000px. All six enemy types appear together from here on. The Gauntlet (x=3,500–5,100) is a tight staircase of 100px platforms — avoid standing on the same platform as a Kamikaze when its fuse is lit.

  • Moving Pillars (x=2,200–2,500) oscillate at different phases. Time the jump between them — waiting for phase alignment is safer than rushing.
  • Hanging Lanterns (x=9,800–10,500) are vertical movers at depth phase offsets; treat them like a rhythm game — jump at the high point of each arc.
  • Save at least one Skunk Shot for the dense cluster in the Upper Ridge (x=11,200–12,500).
  • 3 Golden Idols: Training Hall platform, Upper Walkway, Upper Ridge ledge.
3-2 Dojo Showdown — Boss: BURGER FURY

Burger Fury uses Rapid Strike — the fastest melee output of any boss so far. A moving platform at x=1,100 creates a dodge lane. Pick up the Skunk Shot at x=2,000 mid-fight during his post-attack recovery window if you need range options.

World 4 — Crystal Caverns

4-1 Crystal Caverns

A visually dense 16,000px cave with stalactite vertical movers in the entrance and horizontally oscillating Crystal Chamber platforms mid-run. The Deep Chasm (x=5,600–7,000) is a descending zigzag — fall deliberately to the lower platforms rather than trying to fight up top.

  • The Floating Crystals section (x=9,300–10,000) has three vertically moving platforms. Their combined timing creates a wave — ride one all the way up before jumping to the next.
  • Precarious Path (x=13,000–13,800) has two horizontal movers; they shift in opposite directions, so jump at the moment they pass each other.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Cavern Entrance peak, Underground River platform, Upper Ledges.
4-2 Crystal Showdown — Boss: DR OBSIDIAN

Dr Obsidian's special is Summon — he spawns additional enemies mid-fight. Clear the summons before re-engaging Obsidian; fighting both layers simultaneously is how most players die here. Two moving platforms keep the arena dynamic. The horizontally moving platform at x=2,400 is a consistent escape lane from summon clusters.

World 5 — Crystal Caverns Depths

5-1 Crystal Caverns Depths

No more basic Grunts — the Depths drops you straight into Speedsters, Shield Tanks, Kamikazes, Jumpers, and Throwers. Sinking Columns (x=1,500–2,400) are three vertical oscillators at different phases — treat the safe zone between them as a combat platform and bait enemies onto it.

  • The Echo Chamber (x=5,500–6,600) features horizontal movers over a pit. Fight on the static platform at x=6,400 by baiting enemies there.
  • Crystal Maze (x=9,000–10,000) mixes vertical movers with static platforms — use the static ledge at x=9,400 as a base and let enemies come to you.
  • Three Damage Boosts in this stage (x=3,650, x=10,700, x=14,500) — they stack with Idol bonuses, so grab all three.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Sinking Column platform, Echo Chamber mover, High Ledge Run.
5-2 Depths Showdown — Boss: STEAMPUNK WRAITH

The Wraith teleports, making him the first boss who invalidates your positional read. Watch for the brief pre-teleport flash and stop attacking — let him re-materialize, then punish the arrival animation. Vertical movers at x=1,200 and x=2,300 let you break his targeting after a teleport by changing elevation quickly.

World 6 — Neon Crossroads

6-1 Neon Crossroads

Back to urban terrain with neon aesthetics. Billboard Elevators (x=3,500–4,100) use tall 200px vertical movers — the widest oscillation so far. Let them reach the top before boarding so you get maximum platform time.

  • Crossroads Plaza (x=2,700–3,300) is the widest open platform in the game — perfect for chaining large combos. Funnel enemies onto it before using the elevators.
  • Floating Neon Bars (x=7,200–7,800) are horizontal movers at opposite phase offsets — cross at the closest approach point.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Neon Sign Hop top platform, Rooftop Highway, Antenna Perch.
6-2 Neon Showdown — Boss: ETHEREO NIFTY

Ethereo Nifty fires projectiles and is classified as The Unseen. Stay mobile — standing still is how his projectile pattern converges on you. Use the moving platform at x=1,600 as a platform hop to break his aim. Grab the Speed Boost early to maintain the evasion advantage throughout the fight.

World 7 — Crystal Ridge

7-1 Crystal Ridge

A mountain run with an aggressive enemy spawn rate (1.6s interval, 11 max, 1.05 aggression). The ridge geometry rewards staying on upper platforms — enemies that fall off narrow ledges do your work for you. Use Kamikazes to chain into each other for score bonuses whenever you see two in proximity.

  • 3 Golden Idols: Early ridge ascent, mid-ridge peak, late approach to boss arena.
7-2 Ridge Showdown — Boss: SHADOW FANG

Shadow Fang charges — a telegraphed linear dash across the arena floor. Jump the charge and counter-attack from above. His enrage at 50% HP increases charge frequency significantly. Save a Skunk Shot for the Phase 3 (25% HP) window when he becomes desperate; the 5s pause is enough to deal serious damage safely.

World 8 — Abyssal Caverns

8-1 Abyssal Caverns

Deep cave environment with tight platform spacing and a short spawn interval of 1.4s. The ambient pressure here is constant — keep the kill chain going rather than playing defensively or the screen fills up.

  • Three-boss health multiplier (14×) awaits — farm every Damage Boost this stage, there are two.
  • 3 Golden Idols: Entrance, deep mid section, upper ledge approach.
8-2 Abyss Showdown — Boss: VENOM TOOTH

Venom Tooth charges like Shadow Fang but with a higher health multiplier (14×) and greater attack damage (2.2×). The key difference: his arena is underground — you cannot jump over him if he charges into a wall. Position yourself at least half the arena width away when he winds up for a charge, then dodge to the opposite end.

World 9 — Neon Nexus

9-1 Neon Nexus

The penultimate run. Enemy spawn rate is at 1.2s — the fastest you will face until the Final Showdown. All enemy types appear with maximum aggression (1.4). Stay airborne as much as possible; jumping over clumps of Kamikazes and letting them detonate into each other is the best scoring play here.

  • 3 Golden Idols: Nexus entrance high platform, mid-nexus route, late approach ledge.
9-2 Nexus Showdown — Boss: ASTRAL TYRANT

The Astral Tyrant fires projectiles with a 17× HP multiplier and 2.5× attack damage. His projectile arcs are wider in Phase 2, so the floor becomes a kill zone. Fight from platforms exclusively. Moving platforms at x=1,100 and x=3,400 in the arena provide elevation — use both throughout the fight.

World 10 — Final Showdown

10-1 Final Showdown

The last run stage throws only Kamikazes, Jumpers, and Throwers at you — no grunts, no shields. The spawn rate is 1.2s with maximum aggression (1.5) and up to 8 enemies on screen. Your combo potential here is enormous: Kamikazes chain, Jumpers are high-value, Throwers at 250pts each. Go in with a full Idol set if possible.

  • Grab the Speed Boost at x=850 and Damage Boost at x=2,700 early — they will carry you into the final boss.
  • The arena uses moving platforms at x=1,100, x=2,250, and x=3,400 — the most complex movement puzzle of the campaign.
  • No idols in the boss stage — make sure you cleared all three in 10-1.
10-2 Final Confrontation — Boss: MALODOR

Malodor is the Enemy Kingpin and the true final boss with a 20× health multiplier, 1.3× speed, and 2.7× attack damage. He fires projectiles that cover ground and air, and Phase 2 (below 50%) dramatically increases their density. See the Boss Guide for the full approach.

04

Boss Guide

Every boss has three phases: normal, enraged (below 50% HP), and desperate (below 25% HP). Combat speed and attack frequency increase at each threshold. Always save a Skunk Shot for the transition into Phase 3 — the 5s window is the safest damage opportunity in any boss fight.

KYLE

Crystal Flower Chief World 1-2

Special — Crystal Barrage: Kyle fires a spread of crystal projectiles that fan outward from his position. The spread widens in Phase 2. Stay directly above him on a platform to avoid the fan arc — the crystals travel horizontally.

Phase 1: Slow crystal bursts, predictable timing. Get above and time drops for big damage. Grab the Speed Boost early.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Barrage frequency doubles. Stay on platforms and use jump timing to dodge between bursts. Shadow Strike during his recovery window.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Spray pattern becomes near-continuous. Skunk Shot here to create a safe damage window.

Phase 1: Standard barrage Phase 2: Double frequency Phase 3: Continuous spray

IRON CLAW

Enforcer of Skunk City World 2-2

Special — Ground Slam: Iron Claw leaps and slams the ground, creating a shockwave that travels the arena floor. Jump the slam — both the impact and the wave. He pauses briefly after landing.

Phase 1: One slam per cycle. After the slam, he walks toward you — counter-attack during the walk, not the pause.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Two slams per cycle with a short pause between. Jump both, then punish the second.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Slam speed increases. Use the moving platform at x=1,950 to maintain elevation advantage. A Skunk Shot mid-Phase 3 shuts down his slam cycle entirely.

Phase 1: Single slam Phase 2: Double slam combo Phase 3: Rapid slam flurry

BURGER FURY

Master of the Dojo World 3-2

Special — Rapid Strike: A five-hit melee flurry delivered in quick succession. The tell is a brief stance wind-up before the first hit. Back away the moment you see the stance — jumping away mid-flurry will still clip you if you're too close.

Phase 1: Flurry every 3–4 seconds. Attack two to three times between flurries and back off cleanly.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Flurry becomes a six-hit chain with tighter windows. Stick to a strict attack-and-retreat rhythm.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Constant pressure. The moving platform at x=1,100 is the main dodge tool — use height to bait him into the platform cycle, then drop in behind.

Phase 1: 5-hit flurry Phase 2: 6-hit extended chain Phase 3: Continuous pressure

DR OBSIDIAN

Cavern Sentinel World 4-2

Special — Summon: Obsidian summons two to three standard enemies mid-fight. The adds must die before you re-engage Obsidian or you will be overwhelmed. Summoned enemies are weaker versions but still deal normal damage.

Phase 1: One summon wave. Clear adds quickly with a Shadow Strike sweep, then return to Obsidian during his idle animation.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Summons more frequently and at higher enemy count. The horizontal platform at x=2,400 is your best staging area — stand there and let adds come to you in a line rather than surrounding you.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Nearly continuous summons. Skip the adds in Phase 3 — hit Obsidian hard and fast. He cannot summon while actively attacking.

Phase 1: 1 summon wave Phase 2: Frequent summons Phase 3: Attack through summons

STEAMPUNK WRAITH

Depths Dweller World 5-2

Special — Teleport: The Wraith vanishes and reappears in a new position. He always re-materializes near the player. The arrival animation has a brief vulnerable window (~0.3s) — that is the only consistent damage window he gives you.

Phase 1: Teleport once per engagement cycle. After each teleport, land one or two hits immediately then back off.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Double teleport per cycle — he blinks, attacks, blinks again. Track his glow and don't commit to long combo strings.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Chain teleport. Stay central in the arena and react to the arrival flash. Skunk Shot on arrival freezes him for a full damage burst.

Phase 1: Single teleport Phase 2: Double teleport cycle Phase 3: Chain teleport

ETHEREO NIFTY

The Unseen World 6-2

Special — Projectile: Fires a spread of tracking projectiles. The tracking is not perfect — lateral movement at medium distance confuses the aim. Never stand still or jump in place.

Phase 1: Wide spacing between projectile bursts. Counter-attack between bursts while staying mobile.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Burst frequency increases and projectiles travel faster. The moving platform at x=1,600 is key — change elevation mid-burst to break tracking.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Constant projectile pressure. Shadow Strike toward him between bursts for the shortest possible exposure time.

Phase 1: Tracking spread Phase 2: Fast burst cycles Phase 3: Continuous barrage

SHADOW FANG

Guardian of the Forest World 7-2

Special — Charge: Telegraphed with a brief crouch before a full-arena linear dash. The safe response is to jump the moment you see the crouch — the charge hitbox is low. Land behind him for a free punish window.

Phase 1: One charge per cycle. Consistent pattern — jump, land, punish.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Double charge with a short gap between. Jump both; the second charge can catch you if you land before it fires.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Triple charge at high speed. Skunk Shot into Phase 3 to suppress the charge cycle. Focus damage entirely — the speed in Phase 3 makes prolonged fights risky.

Phase 1: Single charge Phase 2: Double charge Phase 3: Triple high-speed charge

VENOM TOOTH

Ruler of the Underbelly World 8-2

Special — Charge: Like Shadow Fang but 14× health and 2.2× damage. The underground arena walls stop the charge — Venom Tooth bounces off a wall and charges back. Track which wall he hits and move to a third-point position to avoid both the forward and return dash.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Back-and-forth charge combo. The only reliable safe position is directly above him on a platform after the first charge — he cannot reverse mid-dash.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Use Skunk Shot immediately. The 5s freeze is essential at this HP multiplier.

Phase 1: Wall-bounce charge Phase 2: Combo charge Phase 3: Skunk Shot required

ASTRAL TYRANT

Warden of the Void World 9-2

Special — Projectile: Like Ethereo but with 17× HP and wider arcs that cover both floor and midair. The ground is unreliable — use both moving platforms throughout the fight. Never stand at floor level for more than one second.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Platform spacing becomes vital — make sure you understand which mover is ascending before committing to it. A Skunk Shot here gives you the cleanest damage window of the entire fight.

Phase 1: Wide projectile arcs Phase 2: Floor + air coverage Phase 3: Platform-only combat

MALODOR

Enemy Kingpin World 10-2 — FINAL BOSS

Special — Projectile: Malodor fires heavy tracking projectiles with 20× HP, 1.3× speed, and 2.7× attack damage. One hit at low HP can be fatal. He is the fastest boss in the game and his projectiles cover both planes.

Setup: Enter with all three Idols, the Speed Boost from 10-1, and the Damage Boost from x=2,700. Do not skip those pickups — they cut the fight duration significantly.

Phase 1: Predictable single-arc projectile followed by a walk cycle. Counter-attack in the gap between his arc and his next movement step.

Phase 2 (50% HP): Dual projectile arcs with overlapping tracking. Stay on the moving platforms at x=1,100 and x=3,400 — ground fighting in Phase 2 is nearly impossible. Shadow Strike between arc windows only.

Phase 3 (25% HP): Maximum aggression. Deploy the Skunk Shot immediately. Use all remaining combo stack to push maximum damage in the 5s window. If you have no Skunk Shot left, mirror his movement from platform height and wait for a projectile pause to Shadow Strike in.

Phase 1: Single arc Phase 2: Dual overlap arcs Phase 3: Full aggression — Skunk Shot critical
Final boss tip: Malodor's attack damage is 2.7× the base value. A full hit at low HP is 40+ damage. Keep your health above 40 entering Phase 3 or you risk a one-shot.

Combo Multiplier System

Kill within 2 seconds of the last kill to extend the combo. Each kill adds one stack. Hitting multiple enemies with a single attack gives +1 stack per additional enemy hit, plus a flat +150 points per extra target.

Score farming tip: Once you hit the 10× cap, every kill is worth 10× base value + multi-hit bonuses. Use Skunk Shot to paralyze a cluster of 3–4 enemies, then hit them all with one sweeping attack: that's (3 kills × base score × 10) + (2 × 150 multi-hit bonus) in a single button press.

Combo Timing Windows

The standard combo window is 2.0 seconds. Hitting multiple enemies in one attack adds +0.3s per extra enemy to the window, letting a clean sweep extend your streak well beyond 2 seconds. Use this to bridge between enemy clusters that aren't immediately adjacent.

Chain Explosion Bonus

When a Kamikaze's detonation triggers another Kamikaze nearby, you earn +500 points per chain depth. A three-Kamikaze chain is worth +1,500 bonus on top of their base points. In Worlds 8–10 where Kamikazes spawn in groups, deliberately holding off on killing one so it explodes into two others is a high-value play.

Power-Ups & Items

⚡ Speed Boost

+50% movement speed for 8 seconds. Always placed in run stages before boss triggers. On bosses, use immediately at the start of a fight to out-pace their attack patterns in Phase 1.

⚔️ Damage Boost

+75% damage for 10 seconds. Best used with a combo already active — entering the boost window at 20× combo means every hit is dealing roughly 17× base damage. Always grab before boss encounters.

💚 Health Regen

+10 HP per second while active (6s duration, total +60 HP). Drops from enemies at 20% rate. Collect them immediately — they don't stack but they can refill you between bursts.

🏅 Golden Idol

+250 points, +30 HP instant, 2s invulnerability, and a stacking combat bonus. Collect all three for the +2,000 point set bonus plus +25%/+30% speed/damage that carries into the boss fight.

💨 Skunk Shot Ammo

Each pickup grants ammo for the Skunk Shot. Run stages contain two pickups each; boss stages one. Used wisely, each shot is worth a clean Phase 3 damage window on any boss. Never waste a shot on a single low-tier enemy.

❤️ Extra Life

3% drop chance per enemy kill. Do not rely on farming this — keep HP above 40 at all times so any surprise drop is a bonus rather than a necessity.

Survival Mode

Survival Mode puts you in a compact 2,800px arena with no exit and no campaign progression — just escalating enemy waves until you die. It uses its own leaderboard, and score is measured by wave reached rather than raw points.

What Changes in Survival

  • Enemies spawn in structured waves rather than continuous streaming. A wave clears when all enemies are dead. A 4-second rest period follows before the next wave.
  • Enemy types escalate with each wave — later waves mix all six types simultaneously.
  • One life only. When you die, it's over.
  • A rewarded ad (Watch Ad — Revive) can restore you once per run if you choose to use it.
  • No Golden Idols, but Speed Boosts and Damage Boosts appear as the arena rewards.

Survival Strategy

Use the arena walls: Enemies funnel from both sides in Survival. The center of the arena is a kill zone — stand slightly off-center toward one wall, let enemies cluster to your left, then Shadow Strike through them in a sweeping line.
Kamikaze chains are your wave-clear: In later waves, 2–4 Kamikazes may appear. Do not kill them immediately — let them group up by staying mobile, then set off one detonation chain for massive score and fast wave clear.
Shield Tanks block the center: In later waves, Shield Tanks planted in the middle split the arena into two half-width lanes. Hit them from the side or use Skunk Shot to pause their cycle, then clear them before they set up permanently.
