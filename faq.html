Is Skunked free to play?

Yes. The browser version and Android release are free to play, with optional purchases that unlock extra content and support the project.

What platforms does it support?

Skunked runs in modern web browsers and on Android through the mobile build.

Do purchases remove ads?

On supported mobile builds, purchases can unlock ad-free play and premium cosmetics. The web version is still evolving its monetization strategy.

How do I report a problem?

Use the in-game support flow or visit the privacy and terms pages for product information and contact links.
