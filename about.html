About Skunked: Way of the Spray

Skunked: Way of the Spray is a fast, colorful arcade brawler where the Ninja Skunk uses spray-powered attacks, quick combos, and momentum-based movement to survive intense waves of enemies. It is designed for short bursts of play, replayable runs, and an energetic, hand-crafted feel.

What the game is

It is a browser-first and Android-friendly action game focused on simple controls, immediate feedback, and escalating challenge. The core loop rewards aggressive timing, smart positioning, and clean combo execution.

Gameplay pillars

Combo chains, boss pressure, survival runs, and collectible skins form the heart of the experience. Each run is meant to feel distinct, replayable, and easy to pick up again.

Why it exists

The game blends arcade action with a playful punk aesthetic. It is built to be approachable for casual players while still giving dedicated players room to improve and master its rhythm.

Skunked: Way of the Spray is a browser game and Android release from Mephitideus Interactive.
