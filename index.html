Field Manual

                        
📖 How to Play

                        
Everything you need to survive, score big, and master the spray.

                    

                    


                    

                        
🎯 Objective

                        
Fight through 10 stages of enemies and bosses. Defeat the boss in a stage to advance. Lose all your lives and it's game over — your final score is submitted to the leaderboard.

                    


                    

                        
⭐ Scoring

                        
Every enemy you defeat earns base points. Chain kills within 2 seconds to build a combo multiplier (up to 10×). The faster you chain, the higher your score climbs.

                        

                            
Grunt100 pts

                            
Speedster120 pts

                            
Shield Tank150 pts

                            
Kamikaze200 pts

                            
Jumper200 pts

                            
Thrower250 pts

                            
Boss Kill500+ pts

                            
Chain Explosion500 bonus

                        

                        
💡 Boss score scales with their HP multiplier — later bosses are worth more.

                    


                    

                        
👑 Golden Idols

                        
Each stage hides 3 Golden Idols. Collecting one gives you +250 score, +30 HP, and 2 seconds of invulnerability.

                        
Grab all 3 in a single stage to activate the Idol Set Bonus:

                        

                            ✅ +25% Movement Speed
                            ✅ +30% Attack Damage
                            ✅ +2,000 Bonus Points
                        

                        
💡 Idols are placed throughout the level — explore every platform to find them all!

                    


                    

                        
⏱ Time Attack & Pacing

                        
Each stage has a stage timer ticking down. Clear enemies quickly to earn a time bonus at the end of the stage. The faster you clear, the more bonus points you bank.

                        
Speed isn't just about the timer — keeping momentum feeds your combo chain. Hesitate too long between kills and the multiplier resets.

                        
💡 Balance speed with survival — dying costs a life and resets your combo.

                    


                    

                        
⚔ Combat Basics

                        
You have three attack types at your disposal:

                        

                            

                                
🗡 Basic Attack

                                
Fast melee strike. Low cooldown, perfect for building combos. Press X or the Attack button.

                            

                            

                                
⚡ Shadow Strike

                                
Dash forward with invincibility frames. Pass through enemy attacks! Press Z or the Special button.

                            

                            

                                
💣 Skunk Shot

                                
Ranged spray that "skunks" enemies, freezing them for 5s. Ammo-based — collect pickups. Press C.

                            

                        

                    


                    

                        
💛 Survival Tips

                        
    
                            
  • You start with 3 lives and 100 HP. Losing all HP costs a life.
    • 
                            
  • Enemies drop Health Regen (20% chance) and rarely Extra Lives (3% chance).
    • 
                            
  • Use Shadow Strike to dodge through dangerous situations — you're invincible during the dash.
    • 
                            
  • Skunk Shot disrupts Kamikaze fuses — spray a group before they explode!
    • 
                            
  • Bosses telegraph their big attacks — watch the wind-up animation and dodge first, attack second.
    • 
                            
  • Double-jump (tap Space twice) to reach high platforms and dodge Jumper arcs.
    •

📜 Story

                            
In the ancient era, the Skunk Clan were the revered wardens of the Whisperwood, masters of the forbidden Miasma Arts. They maintained the balance between the green world and the spirit realm with their unique, pungent shadow-magic.

                            
But peace has withered. Lord Malodor, a rogue shadow-cat corrupted by industrial greed, has unleashed the Iron Plague. From the deepest caverns to the heights of his neon-soaked fortresses, his robotic legions and mutated beasts have choked the woodland in smog.

                            
You are the last heir to the Stink-Jutsu tradition. As the final Ninja Skunk, you must journey through the corrupted heart of the realm to reclaim the Three Golden Idols of Purity. Only by mastering the lethal Shadow Strike and the legendary Skunk Shot can you purge the toxic corruption and restore the forest's true scent... of freedom.

⚔ Combat Moves

                        

                            

                                
🗡 Basic Attack

                                
Quick melee strike. Fast cooldown, chain rapidly for combos.

                                
DMG: 15 • Cooldown: 0.3s • Range: 120px

                            

                            

                                
⚡ Shadow Strike

                                
Dashing lunge with invincibility frames. Pass through enemy attacks unscathed.

                                
DMG: 15 • Cooldown: 0.45s • Dash: 380px/s • i-frames!

                            

                            

                                
💣 Skunk Shot

                                
Ranged spray projectile. Creates an AoE cloud on impact — “Skunks” enemies for 5s, disabling movement & attacks.

                                
Ammo-based • Cooldown: 0.5s • Speed: 600px/s

👾 Enemy Types

                        

                            

                                

                                

                                    
💀 Grunt

                                    
HP 50 • SPD 150 • 100 pts

                                    
Standard patrol enemy. Chases player within 300px, melee attacks at close range.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
💨 Speedster

                                    
HP 40 • SPD 225 • 120 pts

                                    
50% faster but fragile. Quick points if you can catch them before they overwhelm you.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
🛡 Shield Tank

                                    
HP 75 • SPD 105 • 150 pts

                                    
Slow bruiser with a Shield Bubble (every 2.2s, lasts 1.5s). Time your attacks between shields!

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
💥 Kamikaze

                                    
HP 25 • SPD 300 • 200 pts

                                    
Closes in, ignites a 1.6s fuse, then dashes and detonates! AoE deals 55 damage. Chain explosions = 500 bonus pts. Skunk Shot disrupts the fuse.

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
🚀 Jumper

                                    
HP 60 • Stationary • 200 pts

                                    
Doesn’t walk — performs up to 5 rapid vertical jumps to reach you. Watch for the green sparks!

                                

                            

                            

                                

                                

                                    
🎯 Thrower

                                    
HP 60 • SPD 128 • 250 pts

                                    
Ranged attacker throwing purple spray orbs from 400px. Retreats when you close in. Highest points among regular enemies.
