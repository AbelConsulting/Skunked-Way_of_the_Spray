📜 Story

You are the last Ninja Skunk. Dark forces have invaded the forest, and only your legendary spray technique can save the woodland realm. Battle through 10 stages of increasingly dangerous foes, defeat powerful bosses, and prove you are the ultimate skunk warrior!

⭐ Key Features

10 StagesForests, caverns, neon cities & more
6 Enemy TypesEach with unique AI behaviour
5 BossesMassive health, deadly patterns
Combo SystemChain hits for up to 10× score
3 Attack TypesMelee, Shadow Strike & Skunk Shot
6 Power-upsHealth, lives, speed & damage boosts
5 AchievementsUnlock badges for skilled play
Online ScoresCompete on the global leaderboard

💡 Pro Tips

  • Chain combos within 2 seconds — multiplier caps at 10×
  • Collect all 3 Golden Idols per stage for a huge +30% damage & +25% speed set bonus
  • Shadow Strike grants invincibility frames — dash through attacks!
  • Skunk Shot disrupts Kamikaze fuses — force early detonation on groups
  • Watch boss wind-up animations before committing to attacks
  • Double-jump lets you platform over the Jumper enemy’s arc

⚔ Combat Moves

🗡 Basic Attack

Quick melee strike. Fast cooldown, chain rapidly for combos.

DMG: 15 • Cooldown: 0.3s • Range: 120px

⚡ Shadow Strike

Dashing lunge with invincibility frames. Pass through enemy attacks unscathed.

DMG: 15 • Cooldown: 0.45s • Dash: 380px/s • i-frames!

💣 Skunk Shot

Ranged spray projectile. Creates an AoE cloud on impact — “Skunks” enemies for 5s, disabling movement & attacks.

Ammo-based • Cooldown: 0.5s • Speed: 600px/s

🔥 Combo Tiers

Hit enemies within 2s windows. Multiplier = 1 + (hits × 0.25), max 10×

3+ NICE! 5+ GREAT! 10+ AWESOME! 15+ INCREDIBLE! 20+ UNSTOPPABLE! 30+ LEGENDARY! 50+ GOD MODE!

👾 Enemy Types

💀 Grunt

HP 50 • SPD 150 • 100 pts

Standard patrol enemy. Chases player within 300px, melee attacks at close range.

💨 Speedster

HP 40 • SPD 225 • 120 pts

50% faster but fragile. Quick points if you can catch them before they overwhelm you.

🛡 Shield Tank

HP 75 • SPD 105 • 150 pts

Slow bruiser with a Shield Bubble (every 2.2s, lasts 1.5s). Time your attacks between shields!

💥 Kamikaze

HP 25 • SPD 300 • 200 pts

Closes in, ignites a 1.6s fuse, then dashes and detonates! AoE deals 55 damage. Chain explosions = 500 bonus pts. Skunk Shot disrupts the fuse.

🚀 Jumper

HP 60 • Stationary • 200 pts

Doesn’t walk — performs up to 5 rapid vertical jumps to reach you. Watch for the green sparks!

🎯 Thrower

HP 60 • SPD 128 • 250 pts

Ranged attacker throwing purple spray orbs from 400px. Retreats when you close in. Highest points among regular enemies.
